===== PAGE 3 =====
Sequence diagram: voice-guided 
StoryLearn session
Purpose. It shows, in time order, how the objects of StoryLab Voice exchange messages when 
a student learns through a story. It covers the voice pipeline, the adaptive question loop, 
local saving and the cloud sync.
Participants (lifelines)

===== PAGE 4 =====
Lifeline Role
:Student The actor, who speaks and listens
:VoiceUI Microphone and speaker interface with the 
animated avatar and waveform
:VoiceEngine Speech-to-text (STT) and text-to-speech 
(TTS)
:AIVoiceAssistant The central tutor. It interprets requests 
and routes them.
:StoryLearn The story engine. It narrates, asks 
questions and evaluates answers.
:AdaptiveEngine Adjusts story difficulty
:LocalStore Offline storage (IndexedDB)
:SyncEngine Background synchronization
:CloudServer Cloud server that receives the student's 
progress

Notation
Solid arrows are calls, and dashed arrows are returns or responses.
Activation bars on a lifeline show when that object is active.
Self-arrows are an object calling itself: speechToText() (3, 19), interpretIntent() (5) 
and detectConnection() (35).
Fragments:
loop [until story finished] covers 13–28.
alt [answer correct] and [wrong answer, confused or long pause] cover 24 
and 25.
opt [internet available] covers 35–40.
Requirements covered: FR-CB-01 and FR-CB-02 (voice conversation), FR-CB-03 (hints when 
confused), FR-CB-04 (supportive feedback), FR-SL-01 and FR-SL-02 (story narration and 
questions) and FR-SL-03 (adaptive difficulty). The offline-ﬁrst requirement shows up as the 
local save followed by an optional sync.
Communication diagram: the same 
scenario
Purpose. It shows the same interaction as the sequence diagram, but the focus is on which 
objects are linked, not on time order. The message numbers (1–40) are the same, so each 
message can be matched to the sequence diagram.
Objects and links. The objects are the same nine as the lifelines above. Seven links connect 
them along the main path:
Student – VoiceUI
VoiceUI – VoiceEngine
VoiceEngine – AIVoiceAssistant
AIVoiceAssistant – StoryLearn
StoryLearn – AdaptiveEngine
StoryLearn – LocalStore
LocalStore – SyncEngine

Self-calls (speechToText(), interpretIntent(), detectConnection()) are written inside 
the object boxes with a ↻  symbol. A note at the bottom records the loop (13–28), the 
alternative (24 or 25) and the optional sync (35–40).
Sequence vs communication. The sequence diagram emphasizes when each message 
happens. The communication diagram emphasizes who talks to whom. It makes the 
architecture visible: the assistant sits in the middle, the voice pipeline runs on its left, and the 
story, adaptive and storage objects run on its right.
