System  Modeling:  Use  Case  Analysis  
1.  Actors  (Users)  
●  Student:  Primary  rural  learner  who  uses  voice/touch  to  interact  with  stories  and  virtual  
labs.
 ●  Teacher:  Supervisor  who  assigns  modules,  monitors  student  progress,  and  views  
reports.
 ●  Administrator:  System  engineer  who  manages  user  roles,  AI  voice  models,  and  system  
configurations.
 ●  AI  Voice  Assistant:  Central  intelligent  subsystem  coordinating  speech  recognition,  TTS,  
and
 
core
 
app
 
engines.

●  UC-01:  Interact  via  Voice  Chatbot:  Bidirectional  spoken  tutoring,  Q&A,  and  navigation.  ●  UC-02:  Learn  via  Multi-Subject  Stories  (StoryLearn):  Interactive  audio  story  modules  
with
 
embedded
 
questions.
 ●  UC-03:  Perform  Virtual  Science  Experiment  (LabInBox):  Voice-guided  2D  virtual  lab  
execution.
